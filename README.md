One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new — entered in the [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) writes the entry sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and validates the 1–200 character gate.
